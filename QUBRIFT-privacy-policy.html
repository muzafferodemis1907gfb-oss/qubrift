QUBRIFT Privacy Policy

Effective: 1 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QUBRIFT handles information when you use the Android game. QUBRIFT does not currently provide user accounts or require you to submit your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment information to play.

1. Information stored locally on your device

QUBRIFT stores gameplay information locally using Android/libGDX preferences. This can include scores, high-score history, coins, selected theme and mode, audio settings, daily reward state, career progress, missions, achievements, Rift Shards, contracts, Singularity counts, and advertisement-cycle state. This information is used to provide game features and persistence. It is not uploaded to a QUBRIFT account because the current release has no account system.

2. Advertising and third-party processing

QUBRIFT uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertising. Depending on your region, device settings, consent choices, and Google's services, Google may process information such as IP address (which can be used to estimate general location), device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, advertising interactions, and diagnostic/performance information. Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

Advertising data is handled by Google under Google's own privacy terms. You can review Google's privacy information at Google Privacy Policy and advertising technologies information at How Google uses advertising technologies.

3. Consent and privacy choices

Where required, QUBRIFT uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent and privacy choices. When Google indicates that privacy options must remain available, QUBRIFT provides an in-app entry point under How to Play → Privacy Policy.

4. Rewarded advertisements

Rewarded advertisements are optional. A player may choose to watch a rewarded advertisement to receive an in-game benefit such as a revive or bonus coins. The reward is granted only after the advertising SDK reports successful completion of the rewarded event.

5. Online Arena

The production build currently keeps Online Arena disabled until a production server is configured. If Online Arena is enabled in a future version, the game may transmit a randomly generated player alias and gameplay state such as score, lives, cleared lines, and competitive pressure to the configured QUBRIFT game server. This policy must be updated before enabling a production server if the server stores additional information.

6. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored QUBRIFT gameplay data remains on the device until it is overwritten by gameplay, Android app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. QUBRIFT does not currently maintain a cloud account database. Advertising information processed by Google is subject to Google's own retention and deletion practices and device privacy controls.

7. Security

QUBRIFT uses standard Android networking and the Google advertising SDK. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. No system can guarantee absolute security, but the app is designed to request only the permissions required for networking and advertising.

8. Children's privacy

QUBRIFT is not designed as a service directed to children under 13. The publisher must configure the Google Play target-audience and advertising settings to match the final intended audience. If the target audience changes to include children, the app and this policy must be reviewed for the Google Play Families requirements before release.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when QUBRIFT adds features, changes advertising providers, enables Online Arena, adds cloud accounts, or changes data practices. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
